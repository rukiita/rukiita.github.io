Weird — An archive of weird experiences

Last updated: September 19, 2026 ・ 日本語

Weird is a mobile app for recording and sharing strange experiences — ghosts, UFOs, urban legends — on a map, at the place where they happened. It is available for iPhone.

What you can do

  • Find them on the map. Browse experiences other people have recorded near you, or anywhere you are curious about. No account is needed to read.
  • Record your own. Post from where you are, or drop a pin for something that happened in the past. You can add the date and time, the weather, photos, a tag and your own thoughts on what caused it. You can also post anonymously.
  • Weigh it up. Each experience shows how many similar ones were recorded nearby, how many people have viewed it, and a trust score. Comments let people add what they know.
  • Weird+ (paid). An auto-renewing monthly subscription that unlocks search with filters. A free trial is included. Purchases are made through the App Store, and you cancel from your App Store settings.

Safety features

  • You can report a post or a comment from inside the app.
  • You can block a user so their posts and comments no longer appear.
  • Titles, bodies and cause notes are checked against a banned-word list when you post.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the app's settings.
